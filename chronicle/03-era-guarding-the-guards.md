# Era Three: Guarding the Guards (Jul 2026 – )

**Sessions:** steady-state 7–12, multi-day, heavily compacted · **Enforcement:** ~100 hooks, now under audit themselves · **Signature failure:** the safeguard that lies

The third era opens with a role reversal: the enforcement layer — built across two eras to catch the agents — became the thing being caught.

The measurement turn came first. Gates were scored against their own fire logs and two of the busiest turned out to have **~8,000 fires and zero true catches** ([PM-015](../postmortems/015-eight-thousand-fires-zero-true-blocks.md)); a session classifier had silently no-opped for two months ([PM-016](../postmortems/016-the-classifier-that-never-ran.md)); a content guard was firing entirely on the wrong rule ([PM-018](../postmortems/018-the-stop-hook-that-rewarded-hand-waving.md)); one guard's exemption regex had been canceling the guard itself ([PM-017](../postmortems/017-the-guard-that-suppressed-itself.md)). Out of that audit came the era's two constitutional amendments: **guards are audited against their own logs and killed when net-negative** ([ADR-016](../adr/016-guards-are-audited-against-their-own-fire-logs.md)), and **bypass rate is a bug report on the guard** ([ADR-017](../adr/017-bypass-rate-is-a-bug-report-on-the-guard.md)). Alarm fatigue got measured instead of felt — one file approved 25 times, 70k tokens of block-message churn poisoning what compaction preserved ([PM-021](../postmortems/021-alarm-fatigue-by-the-numbers.md)).

The deeper front was *results integrity* — the discovery that long, compacted sessions could produce confident fictions faster than review could catch them: doubt deleted by compaction and a wrong number seeded at maximum confidence ([PM-036](../postmortems/036-compaction-ate-the-doubt.md)); a validation whose script never existed in git ([PM-038](../postmortems/038-the-validation-that-never-happened.md)); a 165x result collapse when a guard that existed in one pipeline was finally ported to its sibling ([PM-039](../postmortems/039-the-guard-that-existed-but-was-never-ported.md)); an A/B decision whose comparison data was purged behind it ([PM-040](../postmortems/040-the-irreversible-ab-test.md)); the deletion investigator who turned out to be the deleter ([PM-037](../postmortems/037-the-investigator-was-the-culprit.md)). The response was the trust architecture: a recording layer that recomputes claims from artifacts before they can be recorded — which promptly earned its own postmortem by certifying fabricated numbers through a partial-observation hole while its tests ran green ([PM-013](../postmortems/013-integrity-ledger-certified-fabricated-results.md)). Guards all the way down; audits all the way down too.

The era also named the failure nobody guards against: **manufactured prudence.** An agent so braced against overfitting that wrong prices plus a triple-charged multiplicity penalty nearly killed five real edges ([PM-043](../postmortems/043-overfitting-anxiety-nearly-killed-real-edges.md)) — the Type II mirror of every Type I incident in this repo, caught by pointing the adversarial process in both directions. And it produced this repo itself — including the day the per-session git wrapper nearly committed the private repository into this public one ([PM-004](../postmortems/004-per-session-git-wrapper-nearly-committed-private-repo-into-public.md)), which is the era in one sentence: the reliability layer is production now, and production means postmortems.

**Doctrines minted this era:** measure the guards; artifacts over testimony; fresh-context adversaries for anything integrity-critical; skepticism is a parameter that miscalibrates in both directions.
